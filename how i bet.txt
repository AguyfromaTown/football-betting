BETTING STRATEGY — Guarded Quarter-Kelly with Pre-Kickoff Authorization

The old 5-step recovery sequence was replaced because it risked 99%+ bankroll 
destruction on a single 5-bet losing streak (Bet 5 alone was 75% of bankroll). 
The new system is mathematically optimal for long-term growth while capping 
per-bet risk to a maximum of 3%.

HOW IT WORKS

Each authorized bet uses one-quarter of the full Kelly fraction calculated from
the final revalidated odds and calibrated model probability. Grade limits remain
hard maximums, and the minimum authorized stake is 0.5% of bankroll:

  Top Pick (score > 8.0, EV > 8%)    → quarter-Kelly, capped at 3%
  Value Pick (score > 7.0, EV > 5%)  → quarter-Kelly, capped at 2%
  Moderate Pick (score > 5.5, EV > 0%) → watchlist only, no stake
  No Bet (everything else)           → 0% of bankroll

Daily discovery only stages a candidate. No stake is logged or deducted until
the scheduled workflow rechecks it within 90 minutes of kickoff. Odds, EV,
bookmaker coverage, fixture status and model agreement must still pass.
Cancelled, postponed, abandoned, suspended and void fixtures are refunded.

Learning is guarded: calibration needs at least 100 comparable settled
predictions; learned goal/market/form weights need at least 200 observations and
a 3% Brier-score improvement on a chronological holdout. Otherwise the
challenger stays in shadow mode. A competition/side/odds segment is suspended
only after at least 30 results show both ROI below -5% and average CLV below -2%.
Friendlies and youth matches receive a 3-point probability penalty; qualifiers
and playoffs receive a 1.5-point penalty for lineup and motivation uncertainty.

PRE-KICKOFF DATA QUALITY

The final check also requests confirmed ESPN starting lineups, measures rest
days from verified league history, and compares prices across bookmakers. A bet
is cancelled when bookmaker dispersion exceeds 12%, absolute price movement
from discovery exceeds 10%, fewer than two bookmakers remain, or the combined
data-quality score falls below 5/10. Missing lineup data is recorded explicitly
but does not fabricate an injury conclusion.

No more than two bets from the same competition can be authorized in one run.
Team aliases are stored in team-aliases.csv so equivalent names from ESPN,
Odds-API and historical result files resolve to the same club.
Every scheduled check appends its current price, bookmaker count, dispersion and
event status to price-history.csv, producing a full pre-kickoff movement trail.

EXAMPLES

Bankroll €100.00:
  Top Pick bet:    €0.50–€3.00 depending on quarter-Kelly
  Value Pick bet:  €0.50–€2.00 depending on quarter-Kelly
  Moderate bet:    €0.00 (watchlist only)

At the maximum Top Pick stake, a win at 1.55 returns €4.65 and the settled
bankroll becomes €101.65; a loss leaves €97.00. Smaller Kelly stakes move the
bankroll proportionally less.

WHY THIS WORKS BETTER

1. Maximum per-bet risk is 3%, not 75% — a 10-bet losing streak costs ~30% 
   of bankroll (recoverable), not 99%+ (game over).

2. Higher confidence bets get more capital — capital is allocated efficiently 
   to the highest-value opportunities.

3. No forced escalation after losses — every bet is independent. No chasing.

4. Compound growth — winning increases the bankroll, which increases future 
   stakes. Losing does the opposite. This is the same mechanism that makes 
   Kelly Criterion optimal.

5. Easy to calculate — no sequence tables needed. Just multiply current 
   bankroll by the tier percentage.
